## What "read" actually tells you

Answer-first: almost nothing on its own. She may have read it mid-meeting, meant to reply, and forgotten.

## What to do next

1. Do nothing for a day or two.
2. Check whether your last message even needed a reply.
3. Re-open once, fresh. Then let it go.
